The Purpose of Your Giving

Where Your Support Goes

Your giving equips a selected church to share Christ, meet practical needs, and care for people who give their lives to Him.

Gospel Tracts and Evangelism

Funds help the selected church print tracts about Christ, salvation, being born again, and life in Him. The pastor or appointed minister shares the gospel during the joint outreach.

Food, Clothing, and Practical Supplies

The remaining outreach funds help the church prepare food, clothing, water, or other needed items for the people it has been planning to reach.

Follow-Up and Growth in Christ

People who give their lives to Christ are handed over to the partner church for follow-up, fellowship, and discipleship. Our platform checks with church leaders on how these people are growing in faith.

How We Partner With a Church

When a substantial amount has accumulated, our team selects one church registered on the platform. We call its pastor to ask what outreach the church has been planning, then give the church funds for gospel tracts and practical supplies. Our representatives join the church on the agreed day.
