Outreach Focus Areas

How the Foundation Serves Communities

We partner with local verified churches and approved orphanages to deliver tangible holistic support.

Food & Clean Water

Providing nutritious bulk food packs, baby formula, and clean water filtration for orphanages and low-income families.

Clothing & Hygiene Kits

Distributing essential winter coats, shoes, blankets, soap, dental care, and personal sanitation items.

Education & School Supplies

Equipping young students with backpacks, books, stationery, uniforms, and tuition sponsorships.

Medical & Health Care

Assisting with emergency prescription care, first-aid kits, pediatric checkups, and wellness supplies.

Shelter & Emergency Relief

Helping orphanages repair facilities, replace damaged beds, and withstand winter weather emergencies.

Spiritual Care & Hope

Sharing Bibles, children's devotional books, prayer support, and evangelistic mentorship for every child.

For Orphanages & Nonprofits

Need Humanitarian Assistance for Your Center?

Orphanages, community shelters, and vulnerable children's organizations can apply for bi-monthly support (food, school supplies, clothing, facility aid). Every request undergoes verification prior to project approval.

  • Open to registered orphanages and verified ministry centers
  • Transparent review process by platform super-admins
  • Direct funding and supply delivery coordination

Required Information

  1. Organization registration details & leadership contacts
  2. Number & ages of children served
  3. Itemized list of urgent needs and estimated cost
  4. Verification documents & facility photos
